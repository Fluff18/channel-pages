Privacy policy

Channel Uploader · last updated 11 November 2026

Channel Uploader is used only by its owner, to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts. It does not collect information about anyone else.

What it accesses

  • With the owner's permission, Google (YouTube Data API) access to upload videos and read the owner's channel name.
  • With the owner's permission, Instagram and TikTok access to publish or upload the owner's videos and read the account name.

Where it is kept

The sign-in tokens these services issue are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a folder readable only by the owner. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platform being posted to. No analytics, no advertising, no sale or sharing of data.

Google user data

Channel Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube access only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel.

Removing access

The owner can remove access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Instagram in its Apps and websites settings, TikTok in its Manage app permissions settings. Deleting the token folder on the computer removes the stored tokens.
