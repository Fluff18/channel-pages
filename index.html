Channel Uploader

A private tool, not a public service.

Channel Uploader is a small program that the owner of the short-video channels Unmapped, Backtested and Between Us runs on their own computer. It uploads videos the owner made to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, with the title and description the owner approved.

It has no other users, no sign-up, and no website beyond these pages. It only connects to accounts the owner signs in to.

What it does with each platform

  • YouTube: uploads a video to the owner's channel and sets its title, description, and publish time.
  • Instagram: publishes a reel to the owner's professional account, with the AI-generated content label.
  • TikTok: sends a video to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft, which the owner posts by hand.
